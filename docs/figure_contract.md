# Figure contract

**Core conclusion:** Total ecosystem-service value increased by 22.44% from 2018 to 2023, led by recreation and soil retention, while the magnitude of change varied substantially across counties.

**Figure archetype:** Quantitative hero plus supporting grid.

**Backend:** Python (matplotlib) only.

**Target output:** GitHub portfolio previews (PNG) plus editable SVG sources.

**Panel map:**

- hero: direct 2018–2023 county comparison for water-retention value, with regional totals and growth leaders;
- a: regional total in 2018 and 2023;
- b: service composition by year;
- c: county-level percentage change;
- d: service-level percentage change by county.

**Evidence hierarchy:** The English homepage hero provides the fastest county-level comparison; regional change is the headline supporting evidence; service composition explains the aggregate; county and service heatmaps show heterogeneity.

**Statistics:** Descriptive totals and percentage changes only. No uncertainty estimates or inferential tests are available.

**Reviewer risks:** Model-derived values have no uncertainty intervals; two time points do not establish a trend; no geographic map is shown because reusable boundary data are not available.
